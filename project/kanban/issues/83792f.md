## What to fix

Raised by the independent reviewer of card `9f0012` as a non-blocking finding
and accepted alongside it, so it is filed rather than bounced.

`9f0012` added a "No background work left running when you report." section to
the `agentContract` in `lib/sandbox/default.nix`. The instruction to confirm it
reads (around `default.nix:565`):

> Your `report done` **should** state that nothing was left running.

Every other imperative in the contract uses `always` / `never` / `must`. A
`should` in a contract that an agent reads as its working rules is materially
weaker, and this one carries the confirmation that the whole card exists to
obtain.

The reviewer noted the `SKILL.md` worked example compensates by baking the
clause into the required `report done` summary format, which is why this was not
a blocker.

## What to fix

- Raise the modal to `must` so the contract matches the register of its
  neighbours.
- While there, check the two paragraphs `9f0012` added for any other softened
  phrasing relative to the surrounding contract.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The background-work confirmation is stated as a requirement, not a suggestion
- [ ] The new contract text matches the imperative register used elsewhere in `agentContract`
- [ ] The `SKILL.md` worked example still agrees with the contract wording
