## What to build

Two independent in-guest `nix` build/provisioning faults were observed, both
while working card 6a4feb. Neither is related to the yield-mid-build stall; they
are storage/provisioning faults that leave a guest unable to build or test.

**Fault 1 — build-sandbox setup fails instantly (`fd 18/19`).**
On card-560ea5, `build-sandbox` setup failed immediately for the `bench-deps`
derivation with a "getting status of fd 18/19" error — even with
`--option sandbox false`.

**Fault 2 — stacked read-only `/nix/store` overlay.**
On review-560ea5, `/nix/store` had two overlay mounts stacked, with the top one
read-only. That reviewer could not run a single test and had to review by
reading the diff alone.

Both are guest-side storage/provisioning defects. They silently degrade a VM
from "can build and test" to "can only read", which corrupts review quality
without any obvious error to the orchestrator.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Root-cause the `fd 18/19` build-sandbox setup failure for guest-side
      derivations and either fix it or document the workaround.
- [ ] Root-cause the double-stacked read-only `/nix/store` overlay and ensure a
      guest boots with a single writable store overlay.
- [ ] A guest can run `nix develop -c <tests>` after boot without either fault.
