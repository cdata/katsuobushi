## What to build

Sandbox implementors stall unreported when they background a long build and
yield the turn. This was the dominant failure across a recent swarm: 3 of 6
instances stalled this way, and the two *harder* faults (a wedged turn, a dead
transport) only ever occurred on instances that had first idled after a yield.

The mechanism:

- The agent runs `nix develop -c <tests>` in the **background**, posts a
  `working` report, has nothing left to do, and ends its turn.
- The guest's auto-nudges are bounded. A 15-25 minute cold build outlasts them.
- The build completes into an empty room. Nothing is wrong from the drive's
  side, so nothing notifies anyone. `phase: ended-unreported`,
  `lastActivityAt` frozen (observed 33 min on card-34071f, 18 min on
  review-7068c3).

The `working`-before-yield instruction is **actively counterproductive**: it
legitimizes the yield. The armed drive only guarantees the command won't return
early, not that the agent keeps working.

The counter-example is the strongest evidence: card-7068c3 posted zero `working`
reports, blocked in the **foreground** the whole time, tripped the 1500s
watchdog notice, and finished cleanly at 36m39s. It was the only instance that
never stalled, wedged, or lost transport. `progressStallSecs = 1500` is already
sized past the cold build precisely to permit foreground blocking.

## The fix

Change the dispatch instruction (`.dispatch-instructions.md` and/or the
`sandbox` / `project-orchestration` skill text): **run the build/test gate in
the foreground; do not background it; do not `report working` and yield. The
watchdog window covers you.** Reserve backgrounding for genuinely unbounded
work, not the build gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Dispatch directive / skill guidance no longer tells agents to
      `report working` before yielding on a build; it directs foreground
      blocking under the watchdog window.
- [ ] The `progressStallSecs = 1500` window is documented as the thing that
      covers a cold foreground build.
- [ ] A dispatched card that triggers a cold (~15-25 min) build completes and
      reports `done` without an intervening stall.
