## What to build

Document how the orchestrator reconciles a branch that no longer applies, and
make the reconciliation visible on the card.

A branch is reviewed against the tip it was based on. It is integrated onto the
tip as it is then. Another card can land in between, so the two are not always
the same, and the reviewer never saw the interaction.

The orchestrator integrates onto the current tip and reconciles what does not
apply. It uses its judgement. In most cases a reconciliation needs no second
review. When a reconciliation changes what the reviewer agreed to, the card
returns to review. That case is rare and is handled one card at a time.

The part that must not be left implicit is the record. A reconciliation is a
change the reviewer did not read, so the card has to say it happened:

- Write what was reconciled into the card's `## Review notes`.
- Say whether the card returned to review, and why.
- Do this before the card moves to `ready`.

Write this into the orchestrator's landing step in
`plugins/katsuobushi/skills/project-orchestration/SKILL.md`, next to the landing
procedure the card moves through.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The skill describes the reconciliation step as part of landing, and says
      the orchestrator uses its judgement
- [ ] A branch that does not apply cleanly leaves a record on the card naming
      what was reconciled
- [ ] The card says whether it returned to review
- [ ] The skill states the test for returning a card to review: the
      reconciliation changed what the reviewer agreed to
- [ ] The record is written before the card reaches `ready`
