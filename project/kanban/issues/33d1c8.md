## What happened

The launch recipe now clears its pre-boot `phase` marker via
`trap 'rm -f <phase>' EXIT INT TERM`, which covers a clean abort, `set -e`, and
Ctrl-C. It cannot cover `SIGKILL` (or a host power-loss): the marker survives,
and `sandbox status` reports `provisioning (<step>)` indefinitely for an
instance with no VM.

This is the residual of the same defect the trap fixed — much narrower, since
an operator who `kill -9`s a launch generally knows they did, but still a state
the tool reports with unwarranted confidence.

## Suggested fix

Bound the marker's credibility in `summarize`
(`rust/katsuobushi-controller/src/sandbox/status.rs`): treat it as stale past a
generous age (its mtime advances on every phase, so a legitimately slow step
keeps it fresh), or record the launching pid alongside the step and check
liveness. Prefer whichever keeps a genuinely slow 20-minute step reading as
`provisioning`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A marker left by a SIGKILLed launch eventually stops reporting
      `provisioning`.
- [ ] A legitimately long provisioning step (e.g. a multi-minute snapshot)
      still reports `provisioning` throughout.
